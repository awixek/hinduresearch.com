About Hindu Research Portal

Welcome to Hindu Research Portal (hinduresearch.com) — an independent, non-profit educational and research platform dedicated to preserving, organizing, and presenting knowledge from ancient Indian scriptures, Sanatan Vigyan, astronomical sciences, and modern scientific contributions.

Because topics spanning health, astronomy, philosophy, and historical scriptures touch on important aspects of people's lives, we aim to follow evidence-based editorial practices and publicly available quality guidance, while clearly distinguishing between primary evidence, scholarly interpretation, traditional accounts, and modern popular claims.

Our Mission & Vision: To bridge ancient Indian Vedic wisdom with modern artificial intelligence technology, offering free, seamless, and globally accessible digital tools for researchers, students, and spiritual seekers without commercial barriers or paywalls.

1. The Developer's Story & Transparency

Hindu Research Portal is a passion-driven, non-profit project developed and maintained entirely by a single independent developer.

  • No Commercial Influence: This portal accepts no commercial funds, corporate sponsorships, or public donations. It is entirely self-funded and operated purely out of devotion to authentic knowledge sharing.
  • Human Accountability: We believe there should be genuine human responsibility behind a digital resource like this one. If you have questions about how the site is run or its content is put together, you're welcome to reach out directly (see Contact Us below).

2. AI Tools & Scriptural Data

To help reduce inaccuracies from artificial intelligence, our AI-assisted tools are designed to draw on primary classical texts and reputable reference material where possible:

  • Rishi AI (Scripture Assistant): Oriented around primary Vedic literature, including the Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, and well-known traditional commentaries.
  • Ayurveda AI: Oriented around classical Ayurvedic treatises such as the Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita, and Ashtanga Hridaya, alongside general modern botanical references.
  • Ramanujan AI: Focused on ancient Indian mathematics and Vedic astronomy (Jyotisha), inspired by historical figures including Aryabhata, Bhaskaracharya, and Srinivasa Ramanujan.

These are AI-assisted tools, not primary sources in themselves. Rishi AI and Ramanujan AI are educational AI simulations; they are not the historical people they reference, and their responses should not be treated as authentic historical statements or verified quotations. See our Editorial & Research Policy for our approach to AI-assisted content.

3. Key Platform Features

  • Live Sanatan Panchang: Location-based astronomical calculations for Sunrise, Sunset, Shubh Muhurat, and Rahukaal.
  • Curated Tirth Directory: A guide highlighting sacred, historical, and scientific sites across India, with historical notes and Google Maps navigation.
  • Vedic & Modern Scientists Profiles: Educational profiles of ancient visionaries (Sushruta, Aryabhata, Kanada, Charaka) alongside modern scientific pioneers (Ramanujan, Bhabha, C.V. Raman, Sarabhai, Bose).
  • Daily Sanatan Vigyan Quiz: Interactive modules for self-assessment and educational engagement.
  • Yoga & Philosophy Guides: Structured guides on classical yoga practices and philosophical frameworks.

4. Our Research & Editorial Approach

Hindu Research Portal aims to present India's historical, scientific, mathematical, philosophical, and cultural traditions through evidence-based and historically contextualized content. As the site's article base grows, we are applying a consistent editorial framework so that readers can see how a claim is supported, not just what the claim is.

In practice, this means our content pages try to distinguish between:

  • Primary historical evidence — original texts, inscriptions, and reliable translations.
  • Scholarly interpretation — how historians and researchers read and contextualize that evidence.
  • Traditional accounts — knowledge passed down through textual or oral tradition, presented as tradition rather than as independently verified history.
  • Modern popular claims — widely repeated statements that may go beyond what the surviving evidence actually supports.

Where historical evidence is uncertain or actively disputed among scholars, we try to say so directly rather than presenting one interpretation as settled fact. We review and update content using available primary sources, scholarly references, and reputable institutional resources where appropriate. This is an ongoing effort rather than a finished, absolute standard.

For a fuller explanation of our source hierarchy, evidence labels, and corrections process, see our Editorial & Research Policy.

Hindu Research Portal is an independent educational and research-oriented project, not a university, research institute, government institution, medical authority, or peer-reviewed journal. It is also not an official representative of the historical personalities discussed on the site. Our content should be read as a starting point for learning and further reading, not as an authoritative academic source in itself.
