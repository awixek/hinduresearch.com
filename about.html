About Hindu Research & Rishi AI

Welcome to Hindu Research. Our mission is to make authentic knowledge from ancient Hindu and Vedic scriptures accessible to everyone through modern AI technology.

What is Rishi AI?

Rishi AI is an intelligent assistant trained to provide responses based exclusively on verified Hindu texts, including the Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, and Puranas.

Our Vision

To create a trusted, digitally preserved, and easily searchable knowledge base of Sanatan Dharma for researchers, students, and spiritual seekers worldwide.
