About Hindu Research Portal

Welcome to Hindu Research Portal (hinduresearch.com) — an educational and research-driven digital platform committed to presenting authentic knowledge from ancient Indian scriptures, Sanatan Vigyan, astronomical sciences, and modern scientific contributions.

Our Mission: To bridge ancient Vedic heritage with modern technology by providing accurate scriptural answers, verified historical facts, interactive utilities, and travel directories for researchers, students, and spiritual seekers worldwide.

Key Platform Features

  • Rishi AI Scripture Assistant: An advanced AI system trained exclusively to answer queries using primary Hindu scriptures and Vedic literature.
  • 50 Mysterious & Sacred Tirth Directory: A dedicated guide for tourists and researchers showcasing 50 sacred, historical, and scientific sites across India, equipped with verified facts and direct Google Maps navigation.
  • Vedic & Modern Scientists Profiles: Educational insights into ancient Indian pioneers (Acharya Sushruta, Aryabhata, Acharya Kanada, Charaka, Bhaskaracharya) and modern icons (Srinivasa Ramanujan, Homi J. Bhabha, Sir C.V. Raman, Vikram Sarabhai, Satyendra Nath Bose).
  • Live Sanatan Panchang: Location-based real-time calculations for Sunrise, Sunset, Shubh Muhurat, and Rahukaal.
  • Daily Sanatan Vigyan Quiz: Interactive daily quiz modules featuring user achievements, performance badges, and social media sharing options.
  • Spiritual E-Books & Literature Hub: Access to curated educational e-books and research papers on Vedic wisdom.

Our Methodology & Content Quality

We strictly prioritize factual accuracy and scriptural integrity. All content, interactive tools, and astronomical calculations on this portal are cross-referenced with traditional texts, recognized historical records, and precise algorithms.
