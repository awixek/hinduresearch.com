About Hindu Research Portal

Welcome to Hindu Research Portal (hinduresearch.com) — an independent, non-profit educational and research platform dedicated to preserving, organizing, and presenting authentic knowledge from ancient Indian scriptures, Sanatan Vigyan, astronomical sciences, and modern scientific contributions.

Because topics spanning health, astronomy, philosophy, and historical scriptures touch upon critical aspects of human life, our platform strictly adheres to Google's Search Quality Rater Guidelines on Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (E-E-A-T) to ensure transparency, rigor, and absolute integrity.

Our Mission & Vision: To bridge ancient Indian Vedic wisdom with modern artificial intelligence technology, offering free, seamless, and globally accessible digital tools for researchers, students, and spiritual seekers without commercial barriers or paywalls.

1. The Developer's Story & Transparency

Hindu Research Portal is a passion-driven, non-profit project developed and maintained entirely by a single independent developer.

  • No Commercial Influence: This portal accepts no commercial funds, corporate sponsorships, or public donations. It is entirely self-funded and operated purely out of devotion to authentic knowledge sharing.
  • Human Accountability & Transparency: In accordance with Google's YMYL (Your Money Your Life) transparency standards, we believe behind every digital resource there must be genuine human responsibility. 2. AI Models & Scriptural Data Transparency

To eliminate artificial intelligence hallucination and deliver accurate insights, our proprietary AI models are strictly grounded in primary classical texts and verified scientific literature:

  • Rishi AI (Scripture Assistant): Trained exclusively on primary Vedic literature, including the Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, and authoritative ancient commentaries.
  • Ayurveda AI: Sourced directly from classical Ayurvedic treatises such as the Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita, and Ashtanga Hridaya, cross-referenced with modern botanical research.
  • Ramanujan AI: Specialized in ancient Indian mathematics, Vedic astronomy (Jyotisha), and computational algorithm analysis inspired by historical pioneers including Aryabhata, Bhaskaracharya, and Srinivasa Ramanujan.

3. Key Platform Features

  • Live Sanatan Panchang: Location-based real-time astronomical calculations for Sunrise, Sunset, Shubh Muhurat, and Rahukaal.
  • Curated Tirth Directory: A dedicated guide highlighting 50 sacred, historical, and scientific sites across India, equipped with verified historical facts and direct Google Maps navigation.
  • Vedic & Modern Scientists Profiles: Educational insights celebrating ancient visionaries (Sushruta, Aryabhata, Kanada, Charaka) alongside modern scientific pioneers (Ramanujan, Bhabha, C.V. Raman, Sarabhai, Bose).
  • Daily Sanatan Vigyan Quiz: Interactive modules designed for self-assessment and educational engagement.
  • Yoga & Philosophy Guides: Structured guides on classical yoga practices and philosophical frameworks.
