Acharya Kanad

Introduction

Acharya Kanad, also written Kanada or Kaṇāda, occupies an important place in the history of Indian philosophy and ancient theories of nature. He is traditionally regarded as the founder or principal early teacher of the Vaisheshika school, a system that attempted to analyze reality by identifying its basic categories, substances, qualities and forms of motion.

Kanad is especially famous because Vaisheshika developed an atomistic theory of matter. The school proposed that certain physical substances are ultimately composed of extremely small, indivisible units called anu or paramanu in later terminology. This makes Vaisheshika one of the world's important ancient traditions of philosophical atomism.

There is, however, an important historical distinction. Vaisheshika atomism should not be presented as modern atomic physics. Kanad did not have the experimental methods, mathematics, particle detectors, quantum mechanics or nuclear physics that define modern science. His achievement belongs to the history of natural philosophy and philosophical analysis.

Who Was Acharya Kanad?

Kanad's biography is surrounded by traditional accounts, and his exact historical dates are uncertain. He is associated with the ancient Indian intellectual world and with the composition or teaching tradition behind the Vaisheshika Sutra. The name Kaṇāda is traditionally interpreted in several ways, while popular stories sometimes connect it with his supposed habit of examining tiny particles or grains.

Because the surviving evidence is primarily textual and traditional, historians must distinguish between what the philosophical texts establish and later biographical legends. What is secure is the importance of the Vaisheshika system and its sustained influence on Indian philosophical thought.

What Is Vaisheshika?

The word Vaisheshika is connected with the idea of particularity or distinction. The school sought to explain the diversity of the world by classifying reality into fundamental categories called padarthas. Instead of beginning only with abstract metaphysical speculation, Vaisheshika attempted to identify the kinds of entities and relations required to make sense of experience.

The classical system is closely connected with the Nyaya school. Over time, Nyaya and Vaisheshika developed a close intellectual relationship, eventually producing what is often called the Nyaya-Vaisheshika tradition.

The Padarthas: Categories of Reality

The categories are central to understanding Kanad's contribution. In the classical formulation, Vaisheshika identifies six major categories. Later Nyaya-Vaisheshika thought adds a seventh, abhava, or absence.

1. Dravya — Substance

Dravya means substance or entity that serves as a locus for qualities and actions. The Vaisheshika system classically recognizes nine substances: earth, water, fire, air, ether, time, space, self and mind. Of these, earth, water, fire and air are associated with atomic physical constituents in the system.

2. Guna — Quality

Guna refers to qualities that inhere in substances. Examples within the classical framework include color, taste, smell, touch, number, size, separation and other properties. A quality is not treated as an independent substance in the same way as the underlying entity that possesses it.

3. Karma — Motion or Action

Karma in Vaisheshika means action or motion in a technical philosophical sense. It helps explain change and movement in physical things. This should not be confused with the broader ethical meaning of karma found in many other Indian traditions.

4. Samanya — Universal

Samanya refers to universality or commonality. It helps explain how multiple individual things can belong to a common class. The concept became important in Indian debates about classification, language and knowledge.

5. Vishesha — Particularity

Vishesha represents ultimate particularity. It allows fundamentally distinct entities to remain numerically different even when they share general characteristics.

6. Samavaya — Inherence

Samavaya is a distinctive relation used to explain inseparable connection, such as the relation between a whole and its parts or between a substance and certain properties. It became one of the most sophisticated relational concepts in the Nyaya-Vaisheshika philosophical system.

7. Abhava — Absence

Later Nyaya-Vaisheshika thinkers recognized abhava, or absence/non-existence, as an additional category. This illustrates how philosophical systems can develop over time rather than remaining fixed in a single historical form.

Kanad's Atomic Theory

The most famous part of Vaisheshika natural philosophy is its account of atoms. The system proposes that certain physical substances are ultimately composed of minute units that cannot be divided indefinitely. These units are generally described as anu, with paramanu becoming an important term in later discussion.

In the Vaisheshika framework, the relevant atoms are associated with earth, water, fire and air. Atoms are eternal and indivisible within the system, while ordinary visible objects are composite. The properties of larger things are explained through the combinations and arrangements of their underlying constituents.

This is philosophically significant because it addresses a classic problem: if every physical object can be divided into smaller parts, does division continue forever, or must there eventually be fundamental units? Vaisheshika answers by positing a limit to physical divisibility.

How Atoms Combine

Vaisheshika atomism is not simply the claim that matter consists of tiny particles. It also attempts to explain how ordinary objects arise from combinations of basic units. Classical discussions describe combinations such as dyads and larger aggregates, although details vary across textual and commentarial traditions.

The philosophical goal is to move from invisible constituents to observable objects. Differences between substances and their qualities are explained through different types and combinations of atoms rather than by treating every visible object as fundamentally unrelated.

The comparison is useful, but the two concepts should not be treated as identical. Saying that Kanad “discovered the modern atom” would project a contemporary scientific concept backward into an ancient philosophical system.

Was Kanad the First Person to Propose Atoms?

There is no simple global “first” that can be established with certainty. Ancient atomistic ideas arose independently in different intellectual traditions. Greek philosophers such as Leucippus and Democritus developed their own atomist arguments, while Indian philosophical traditions including Vaisheshika developed distinctive accounts of indivisible constituents.

The historical importance of Kanad therefore does not depend on proving that he was the world's first atomist. His importance lies in the systematic form of the Vaisheshika framework and its long influence on Indian philosophy.

Vaisheshika and Logic

Vaisheshika was not an experimental science in the modern laboratory sense. It was part of a wider philosophical culture concerned with valid knowledge, inference, classification and explanation. Its later interaction with Nyaya strengthened the role of logical analysis and epistemology.

The combination of ontological categories and methods of reasoning made Nyaya-Vaisheshika one of the most influential analytic traditions in classical Indian philosophy.

Matter, Change and Permanence

Vaisheshika atomism addresses a deep philosophical problem: how can things change while something fundamental remains stable? Composite objects can arise and disappear, while the basic atoms are treated as eternal. Destruction of an object is therefore not absolute annihilation of all constituents; rather, the arrangement of constituents changes.

This distinction between permanent fundamental entities and changing composites gave Indian philosophers a framework for discussing causation, transformation and continuity.

The Role of Time and Space

Vaisheshika's ontology includes kala (time) and dik (space/direction) among the substances. This is notable because the system did not restrict its analysis of reality to visible material things. It also attempted to account for the conditions under which events can occur and objects can be distinguished.

Ether, or akasha, is another substance in the classical system and is associated with sound. These concepts belong to Vaisheshika's philosophical ontology and should not be equated directly with modern electromagnetic fields or physical spacetime.

Mind and Self

Vaisheshika includes atman, or self, and manas, mind, in its list of substances. The mind is treated as a distinct, extremely subtle entity involved in cognition, while the self is associated with consciousness and experience in the broader philosophical system.

This demonstrates that Vaisheshika was not merely a theory of physical particles. It was a complete philosophical attempt to classify both physical and non-physical aspects of reality.

Kanad and Other Indian Knowledge Traditions

Kanad's intellectual legacy belongs to a much larger ecosystem of Indian scholarship. Indian philosophers debated logic, language, metaphysics, mathematics, medicine and astronomy across centuries. Vaisheshika's distinctive contribution was its detailed ontological classification and atomistic explanation of physical composition.

The later relationship between Vaisheshika and Nyaya is especially important. Nyaya developed a sophisticated theory of inference and knowledge, while Vaisheshika supplied a detailed ontology. Their combination created a powerful analytical framework for discussing both what exists and how human beings can know it.

Why Kanad Matters in the History of Science

The history of science is not only a history of modern laboratories. It also includes the history of questions: What is matter? Can matter be divided forever? What makes one object different from another? What is change? How do properties belong to things? What counts as a valid explanation?

Kanad and the Vaisheshika tradition addressed these questions with remarkable systematic ambition. Even where their conclusions differ radically from modern physics, their method of constructing categories and analyzing physical reality is historically important.

Modern Relevance and Limitations

Today, Vaisheshika is valuable for historians of philosophy, historians of science and students of Indian intellectual traditions. It demonstrates that atomistic reasoning was not exclusive to ancient Greece and that South Asian thinkers developed their own detailed accounts of matter and reality.

At the same time, historical scholarship should resist two extremes: dismissing ancient atomism as meaningless because it is not modern physics, or claiming that it predicted every later discovery in particle physics. The strongest historical position recognizes the sophistication of the ancient system while preserving the distinction between philosophical atomism and experimentally tested modern science.

Conclusion

Acharya Kanad's enduring importance lies in the Vaisheshika philosophical tradition. Through its categories of substance, quality, motion, universal, particularity and inherence, the system attempted to construct a coherent map of reality. Its atomism further proposed that certain physical substances are composed of extremely small fundamental units.

Kanad should therefore be remembered neither as a modern nuclear physicist born thousands of years too early nor as merely a speculative philosopher. He was a major figure in an ancient analytical tradition that asked fundamental questions about matter, change, classification and knowledge. That is precisely why his work remains important in the global history of ideas.

परिचय

आचार्य कणाद भारतीय दर्शन और प्राचीन प्रकृति-विचार के इतिहास में एक महत्वपूर्ण नाम हैं। उन्हें परंपरागत रूप से वैशेषिक दर्शन का प्रमुख प्रारंभिक आचार्य माना जाता है। वैशेषिक दर्शन ने पदार्थ, गुण, कर्म, सामान्य, विशेष और समवाय जैसी श्रेणियों के माध्यम से वास्तविकता को व्यवस्थित रूप से समझने का प्रयास किया।

कणाद की सबसे प्रसिद्ध विशेषता उनका परमाणुवाद है। वैशेषिक परंपरा के अनुसार भौतिक पदार्थों को अत्यंत सूक्ष्म और अविभाज्य इकाइयों यानी अणु के माध्यम से समझाया जा सकता है। यह प्राचीन भारतीय atomistic philosophy का एक महत्वपूर्ण उदाहरण है।

लेकिन इसे आधुनिक atomic physics के समान नहीं मानना चाहिए। कणाद के समय आधुनिक प्रयोगशाला, quantum mechanics, particle physics या nuclear physics उपलब्ध नहीं थे। उनका योगदान मुख्यतः natural philosophy और philosophical analysis के क्षेत्र में था।

कणाद कौन थे?

कणाद के जीवन की निश्चित ऐतिहासिक जानकारी बहुत सीमित है। उनकी सटीक तिथियां निश्चित नहीं हैं और उनके बारे में कई पारंपरिक कथाएं मिलती हैं। उन्हें वैशेषिक सूत्र की परंपरा से जोड़ा जाता है।

इसलिए ऐतिहासिक अध्ययन में text और later legends के बीच अंतर करना जरूरी है। जो बात स्पष्ट है, वह वैशेषिक दर्शन का दीर्घकालिक प्रभाव और उसका व्यवस्थित philosophical framework है।

वैशेषिक दर्शन क्या है?

वैशेषिक दर्शन ने दुनिया में मौजूद वस्तुओं और उनके संबंधों को fundamental categories यानी पदार्थ के रूप में classify करने का प्रयास किया। इसका उद्देश्य यह समझना था कि अलग-अलग वस्तुएं, उनके गुण और उनके परिवर्तन किस प्रकार explain किए जा सकते हैं।

समय के साथ वैशेषिक और न्याय दर्शन का गहरा संबंध बना और Nyaya-Vaisheshika एक प्रमुख analytical philosophical tradition बन गया।

सात पदार्थ

शास्त्रीय वैशेषिक में छह मुख्य categories मानी गईं, जबकि बाद की न्याय-वैशेषिक परंपरा में अभाव को सातवीं category के रूप में स्वीकार किया गया।

द्रव्य: substance या वह entity जिसमें गुण और कर्म स्थित हो सकते हैं। वैशेषिक में पृथ्वी, जल, अग्नि, वायु, आकाश, काल, दिशा, आत्मा और मन जैसे द्रव्यों की चर्चा है।

गुण: किसी द्रव्य के properties, जैसे रंग, स्वाद, गंध, स्पर्श, संख्या आदि।

कर्म: motion या action की दार्शनिक category। इसे ethical karma के सामान्य अर्थ से अलग समझना चाहिए।

सामान्य: commonality या universal, जिससे कई individual objects को एक class में समझा जा सकता है।

विशेष: ultimate particularity, जो fundamental entities की individuality को explain करती है।

समवाय: inseparable relation, जैसे whole और parts या कुछ properties और उनके substratum के बीच संबंध को समझाने वाली category।

अभाव: absence/non-existence, जिसे बाद की Nyaya-Vaisheshika परंपरा ने additional category के रूप में स्वीकार किया।

कणाद का परमाणुवाद

वैशेषिक दर्शन का सबसे प्रसिद्ध भाग उसका atomistic model है। इसके अनुसार कुछ भौतिक पदार्थ अत्यंत सूक्ष्म units से बने हैं जिन्हें अणु कहा जाता है। ये fundamental units classical system में indivisible और eternal माने जाते हैं।

वैशेषिक परंपरा में पृथ्वी, जल, अग्नि और वायु से जुड़े atomic constituents की चर्चा मिलती है। दिखाई देने वाली चीजें composite होती हैं और उनके निर्माण को atoms के combinations के माध्यम से explain किया जाता है।

यह विचार एक पुराने philosophical problem का उत्तर देता है: यदि हर वस्तु को लगातार divide किया जा सकता है, तो क्या division अनंत तक चलेगा? वैशेषिक का उत्तर है कि किसी स्तर पर fundamental indivisible units होने चाहिए।

अणु और Modern Atom में अंतर

वैशेषिक का अणु modern atom के समान नहीं है। Modern atom experimental science, chemistry और quantum physics के framework में define होता है। उसके nucleus, electrons और subatomic structure का अध्ययन modern science में किया गया है।

इसलिए “कणाद ने modern atom discover किया” कहना historically बहुत strong claim है। सही बात यह है कि उन्होंने ancient philosophical atomism की एक sophisticated tradition को develop किया।

क्या कणाद दुनिया के पहले Atomist थे?

किसी एक व्यक्ति को पूरी दुनिया का first atomist साबित करना कठिन है। Ancient Greek tradition में Leucippus और Democritus ने भी atomistic arguments विकसित किए। भारत में वैशेषिक परंपरा ने अपना अलग atomistic framework बनाया।

कणाद का महत्व केवल “पहले” होने के दावे में नहीं, बल्कि उनके system की systematic nature और उसके लंबे philosophical influence में है।

न्याय और वैशेषिक

वैशेषिक केवल physical particles की theory नहीं थी। यह knowledge, inference, classification और explanation जैसे philosophical questions से भी जुड़ी थी। बाद में Nyaya के साथ इसके संबंध ने logic और epistemology को और मजबूत किया।

पदार्थ, परिवर्तन और स्थायित्व

वैशेषिक atomism यह समझाने का प्रयास करता है कि composite objects बदल सकते हैं, जबकि fundamental atoms eternal हो सकते हैं। किसी वस्तु का destruction उसके सभी constituents के absolute annihilation के समान नहीं है; arrangement बदल सकता है।

काल, दिशा और आकाश

वैशेषिक ontology में time यानी काल और space/direction यानी दिक को भी substance के रूप में discuss किया गया है। आकाश भी एक distinct substance है, जिसे sound से संबंधित माना जाता है। इन concepts को modern spacetime या electromagnetic field का exact equivalent नहीं मानना चाहिए।

मन और आत्मा

वैशेषिक में mind यानी मन और self यानी आत्मा को भी ontology का हिस्सा माना गया है। इससे स्पष्ट होता है कि कणाद की philosophical system केवल physical matter तक सीमित नहीं थी, बल्कि reality की व्यापक classification प्रस्तुत करती थी।

मिथक और प्रमाण

दावा: कणाद ने modern atom discover किया। स्थिति: बहुत strong claim; उन्होंने ancient philosophical atomism दिया, modern atomic physics नहीं।

दावा: कणाद nuclear physics जानते थे। स्थिति: इसका कोई आधार नहीं है। Nuclear physics बहुत बाद की scientific development है।

दावा: वैशेषिक में atomic theory थी। स्थिति: हां, यदि atomic theory को philosophical atomism के अर्थ में समझा जाए।

दावा: वैशेषिक ने reality को categories में systematically classify किया। स्थिति: यह system की केंद्रीय विशेषता है।

भारतीय ज्ञान परंपरा में कणाद

कणाद की परंपरा भारतीय दर्शन की बड़ी intellectual ecosystem का हिस्सा है। भारत में logic, language, metaphysics, mathematics, medicine और astronomy पर अलग-अलग परंपराओं में चर्चा हुई। वैशेषिक की खास पहचान matter और reality की detailed classification और atomistic explanation है।

न्याय और वैशेषिक का synthesis विशेष रूप से महत्वपूर्ण है। न्याय ने inference और valid knowledge पर sophisticated analysis दिया, जबकि वैशेषिक ने ontology का detailed framework दिया।

आज कणाद क्यों महत्वपूर्ण हैं?

कणाद का अध्ययन history of philosophy, history of science और Indian intellectual traditions के लिए महत्वपूर्ण है। इससे पता चलता है कि atomistic reasoning केवल Greek tradition तक सीमित नहीं थी। South Asia में भी matter और reality पर systematic philosophical analysis विकसित हुआ।

सही historical approach दो extremes से बचती है: ancient atomism को meaningless मानना और यह दावा करना कि उसने modern particle physics की सभी discoveries predict कर दी थीं। वास्तविक महत्व दोनों के बीच है।

निष्कर्ष

आचार्य कणाद की सबसे बड़ी विरासत वैशेषिक दर्शन है। पदार्थ, गुण, कर्म, सामान्य, विशेष और समवाय जैसी categories के माध्यम से इस दर्शन ने reality का एक systematic map बनाने का प्रयास किया। इसके atomism ने physical matter के fundamental units की कल्पना प्रस्तुत की।

कणाद को modern nuclear physicist की तरह प्रस्तुत करना भी गलत है और उनके दर्शन को केवल speculative idea मानकर dismiss करना भी। वे एक ऐसी प्राचीन analytical tradition के महत्वपूर्ण आचार्य थे जिसने matter, change, classification और knowledge जैसे fundamental questions पर गहराई से विचार किया।

Introduction

Acharya Kanad, jise Kanada ya Kaṇāda bhi likha jata hai, Indian philosophy aur ancient natural philosophy ke history mein ek important figure hain. Traditionally unhe Vaisheshika school ka major early teacher maana jata hai.

Kanad sabse zyada apne atomistic theory ke liye famous hain. Vaisheshika ke according physical substances ko extremely small indivisible units, yani anu, ke through explain kiya ja sakta hai. Ye ancient Indian philosophical atomism ka ek important example hai.

But ek important distinction zaroori hai: Vaisheshika atomism modern atomic physics nahi hai. Kanad ke paas modern experiments, quantum mechanics, particle detectors ya nuclear physics nahi thi. Unka contribution natural philosophy aur systematic philosophical analysis mein tha.

Kanad Kaun The?

Kanad ki exact historical dates certain nahi hain. Unka naam Vaisheshika Sutra tradition se associated hai. Unke around kai later traditional stories bhi milti hain, isliye historical evidence aur legends ko separate karna important hai.

Vaisheshika Kya Hai?

Vaisheshika reality ko fundamental categories ya padarthas mein classify karne ki koshish karta hai. Question ye tha ki substances, qualities, motion, individuality aur relationships ko logically kaise explain kiya jaye.

Later Vaisheshika ka Nyaya school ke saath close relationship develop hua aur Nyaya-Vaisheshika ek major analytical philosophical tradition ban gaya.

Padarthas

Dravya: substance, jisme qualities aur actions exist kar sakte hain.

Guna: qualities jaise color, taste, smell, touch, number etc.

Karma: action ya motion ki philosophical category. Isse ethical karma ke general meaning se confuse nahi karna chahiye.

Samanya: universality ya commonality, jo objects ko classes mein samajhne mein help karta hai.

Vishesha: ultimate particularity, jo fundamental entities ki individuality explain karti hai.

Samavaya: inseparable relation, jaise whole-parts ke relation ko explain karne wali category.

Abhava: absence/non-existence, jo later Nyaya-Vaisheshika tradition mein additional category bani.

Kanad Ki Atomic Theory

Vaisheshika ka sabse famous part iska atomism hai. System ke according certain physical substances extremely small units se composed hain, jise anu kaha jata hai. Ye classical system mein indivisible aur eternal maane jate hain.

Earth, water, fire aur air se associated atomic constituents ki discussion milti hai. Visible objects composite hote hain aur unki formation ko combinations ke through explain kiya jata hai.

Is theory ka philosophical question interesting hai: agar matter ko endlessly divide karte rahen, to kya division kabhi end hoga? Vaisheshika kehta hai ki fundamental indivisible units honi chahiye.

Anu vs Modern Atom

Vaisheshika anu ko modern atom ke equal nahi maana ja sakta. Modern atom chemistry aur quantum physics ke experimentally tested framework ka part hai. Uske nucleus, electrons aur subatomic structure ka modern science mein detailed study hua hai.

Isliye “Kanad discovered the modern atom” kehna historically inaccurate hai. Better statement hai ki Kanad ne ancient philosophical atomism ki ek systematic tradition develop ki.

Kya Kanad First Atomist The?

World history mein ek single “first atomist” establish karna difficult hai. Greek thinkers Leucippus aur Democritus ne bhi atomistic arguments develop kiye. Indian Vaisheshika tradition ne independently apna distinct atomistic framework develop kiya.

Kanad ki importance sirf first hone mein nahi, balki Vaisheshika system ki systematic nature aur uske long-term influence mein hai.

Nyaya-Vaisheshika Aur Logic

Vaisheshika sirf particles ki theory nahi thi. Ye knowledge, inference, classification aur explanation jaise questions se bhi related thi. Nyaya ke saath interaction ne logic aur epistemology ko aur sophisticated banaya.

Matter, Change Aur Permanence

Vaisheshika ye explain karta hai ki composite objects change aur destroy ho sakte hain, jabki fundamental atoms eternal ho sakte hain. Kisi object ka destruction necessarily all constituents ke absolute destruction ka meaning nahi rakhta; arrangement change ho sakta hai.

Time, Space Aur Akasha

Vaisheshika ontology mein time (kala), direction/space (dik) aur akasha bhi substances ke roop mein discuss hote hain. Inhe modern spacetime ya electromagnetic fields ka exact equivalent nahi samajhna chahiye.

Mind Aur Self

Vaisheshika mein manas aur atman bhi reality ki classification ka part hain. Isse pata chalta hai ki Kanad ka system sirf physical matter tak limited nahi tha; woh complete ontology develop karne ki koshish thi.

Myths vs Evidence

Claim: Kanad modern atom discover kar chuke the. Assessment: Too strong; unka atomism philosophical tha.

Claim: Kanad nuclear physics jaante the. Assessment: Unsupported.

Claim: Vaisheshika mein atomism tha. Assessment: Yes, philosophical atomism ke sense mein.

Claim: Vaisheshika reality ko systematically classify karta tha. Assessment: Ye school ki central feature hai.

Kanad Ki Legacy

Kanad Indian intellectual history ke ek larger ecosystem ka part hain jisme logic, metaphysics, language, mathematics, medicine aur astronomy ki different traditions develop hui. Vaisheshika ki unique contribution reality ki detailed classification aur atomistic explanation thi.

Nyaya aur Vaisheshika ka synthesis especially important hai: Nyaya ne inference aur valid knowledge ko analyze kiya, jabki Vaisheshika ne ontology ko systematically organize kiya.

Modern Relevance

Aaj Kanad ka work history of philosophy aur history of science ke liye valuable hai. Ye show karta hai ki atomistic reasoning ancient Greece tak limited nahi thi. India mein bhi matter aur reality par systematic philosophical thinking develop hui.

Balanced historical view ye hai ki ancient atomism ko seriously study kiya jaye, lekin usse modern particle physics ki complete prediction bhi na maana jaye.

Conclusion

Acharya Kanad ki lasting legacy Vaisheshika philosophy hai. Substance, quality, motion, universal, particularity aur inherence jaise categories ke through unki tradition ne reality ka systematic map banane ki koshish ki. Atomism ne physical matter ke fundamental units ko explain karne ka model diya.

Kanad ko modern nuclear physicist kehna inaccurate hoga, lekin unki philosophical contribution ko underestimate karna bhi galat hoga. Woh ek major ancient analytical tradition ke representative the jo matter, change, classification aur knowledge jaise fundamental questions par deeply reason karti thi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who was Acharya Kanad?

Kanad was the traditional early teacher associated with Vaisheshika, an Indian philosophical school that developed a detailed theory of categories and atomism.

What is Kanad famous for?

He is chiefly associated with Vaisheshika philosophy and its theory of extremely small fundamental units called anu.

Did Kanad invent the atomic theory?

He is an important early figure in atomistic philosophy, but his ideas should not be equated with modern atomic theory.

What is Vaisheshika?

Vaisheshika is an ancient Indian school that classified reality into padarthas and developed theories of substances, qualities, motion and atoms.

What does anu mean?

Anu is an extremely small fundamental unit in the Vaisheshika account of physical matter.

What are the padarthas?

The classical categories include substance, quality, motion, universal, particularity and inherence; later tradition adds absence.

Did Kanad know modern chemistry or nuclear physics?

No evidence supports that claim. His atomism belongs to an ancient philosophical framework.

When did Kanad live?

His exact dates are uncertain, and scholarly dating of the early Vaisheshika tradition varies.

What was Kanad's contribution to science?

His major contribution was a systematic philosophical account of reality and an atomistic model of physical matter.

Why is Kanad important today?

He is important to the global history of philosophy and science because Vaisheshika represents a major ancient tradition of atomistic and categorical analysis.
