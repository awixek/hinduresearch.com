Mimansa

As one of the six major _Astik darshanas, Mimansa_ is a well-recognized school of Indian philosophy. Its nature is not philosophical advocacy. Rather, as a logical system of interpretation of Veda for carrying out the Vedic rituals, it has practical applications. The word _Mimansa_ derives from the root _man_ meaning “to know”. The _Mimansa_ system is oriented toward interpreting Vedic texts used
