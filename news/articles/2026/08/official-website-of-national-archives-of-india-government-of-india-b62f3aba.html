Unveiling India's Rich Archival Heritage: The National Archives of India

The National Archives of India serves as the primary custodian of the government's enduring records, boasting an extensive collection of historical documents and manuscripts. Established in 1891, it has evolved into the largest archival repository in South Asia. The archives provide access to a vast array of records, including public and private papers, oriental records, and cartographic materials.

Research Brief

The National Archives of India serves as the primary custodian of the government's enduring records, boasting an extensive collection of historical documents and manuscripts. The archives provide access to a vast array of records, including public and private papers, oriental records, and cartographic materials.

Key Takeaways

  • The National Archives of India was established on 11 March 1891 as the Imperial Records Department
  • It holds a vast corpus of records, including public records, private papers, and oriental records
  • The archives provide online access to historical documents and manuscripts through its digital exhibitions and online records

Why This Matters

The National Archives of India plays a crucial role in preserving India's historical heritage, offering invaluable resources for researchers and scholars. Its extensive collection and digital accessibility make it an essential institution for understanding India's rich cultural and historical landscape.
