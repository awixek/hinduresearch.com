Research Brief

This research proposes a Spiral Pedagogical Model to incorporate Indian Knowledge Systems into education, from early childhood to adult learning, by drawing from the Gurukul system's emphasis on experiential learning and holistic development. The model aims to provide a relational and humanizing approach to education in a digital age. By analyzing existing literature, the study seeks to understand how traditional Indian knowledge systems can be integrated into modern education

Key Takeaways

  • The Gurukul system's pedagogical elements, such as experiential education and guru-shishya Parampara, are relevant in modern education settings
  • The Spiral Pedagogical Model integrates Indian Knowledge Systems into education, focusing on holistic development and community-based learning
  • The research utilizes a qualitative and descriptive strategy, analyzing secondary data from peer-reviewed journal articles, books, and government papers

Why This Matters

This research is significant for Indian civilisation and heritage as it attempts to revitalize traditional knowledge systems and incorporate them into modern education, promoting a more holistic and humanizing approach to learning. By exploring the integration of Indian Knowledge Systems, this study contributes to the ongoing efforts to redevelop Bharatiya education and understand its aspirations

Research Hook

A potential future research article could explore the practical applications of the Spiral Pedagogical Model in Indian educational institutions, examining its effectiveness in promoting holistic development and community-based learning among students
