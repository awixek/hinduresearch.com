Research Brief

The National Archives of India has made significant strides in digitizing its vast repository of records, including declassified files relating to Netaji, to facilitate research and public access. The archives' online portal, Abhilekh Patal, provides a comprehensive search platform for scholars and users. The National Archives of India is the largest archival repository in South Asia, housing a diverse range of records and materials

Key Takeaways

  • The National Archives of India has digitized 100 files relating to Netaji, making them accessible to the public and scholars
  • The Abhilekh Patal web portal was launched in 2015 to provide a search platform for archival records
  • The archives hold a vast corpus of records, including public records, private papers, and oriental records, constituting an invaluable source of historical information

Why This Matters

The National Archives of India's digital initiatives are crucial for preserving and promoting India's rich cultural and historical heritage, and for facilitating research and scholarship in the field. By making these records accessible, the archives are enabling a deeper understanding of India's past and its significance in the present
