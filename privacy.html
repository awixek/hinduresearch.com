Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

At Hindu Research Portal (hinduresearch.com), we respect the privacy of everyone who visits the site. This page explains, in plain language, what information is collected when you use the portal, how it is used, and the choices available to you. We are an independent, single-developer educational project — this policy describes our actual practices rather than a formal legal compliance program.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only what is needed to run specific features of the portal. What is collected depends on which features you actually use:

  • Usage & Analytics Data: General information about how visitors use the site (see Section 2, Google Analytics), collected automatically by our analytics tool.
  • Location Data (Panchang tool only): If you use the Panchang feature, your device's browser location may be used to calculate sunrise/sunset and related timings for your area. This is used for the calculation itself; we do not separately collect or sell precise location data.
  • Contact Form Submissions: If you use our contact form, we receive whatever you voluntarily submit — typically your name, email address, inquiry type, and message (see Section 6, Contact Form & Formspree).
  • Sign-In Information (Rishi AI / Quiz): If you sign in to use Rishi AI chat or join the quiz's global leaderboard, we may collect an email address (only if you choose Google sign-in) or nothing more than an anonymous session identifier (if you use anonymous/demo sign-in). See Section 3.
  • Quiz Display Name & Scores: If you enter a name to join the daily quiz leaderboard, that name (which you choose and does not have to be your real name), along with your quiz scores, streak, and play count, may be stored and shown on a public leaderboard. See Section 3.
  • Rishi AI Chat Messages: If you chat with Rishi AI, the message you type and a short recent portion of that conversation are sent to our backend to generate a reply. See Section 3.
  • Locally Stored Preferences: Some features (language choice, light/dark theme, quiz stats, daily play status) are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device — see Section 4.

We do not knowingly collect payment information, government ID numbers, phone numbers, physical addresses, or other sensitive personal data through the portal.

2. Google Analytics (GA4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID: G-D1K553SJT4) to understand traffic and usage patterns, measure performance, and improve the site. Depending on your browser and device settings, Google Analytics may process information such as:

  • Aggregate visitor and session statistics
  • Approximate (city/region-level) geolocation derived from IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages viewed and general interaction/usage information
  • First-party identifiers and cookies used by Google Analytics, such as _ga, where applicable

We have not enabled Google Signals, Google Ads integration, remarketing, or advertising personalization on this property, and we do not currently run Google Ads. If this changes, this policy will be updated accordingly.

You can learn more about how Google Analytics collects and processes data, and about your choices, at:

  • How Google Analytics data is collected and processed
  • Data retention in Google Analytics
  • Google Analytics Safeguarding Your Data

3. Sign-In, Quiz Leaderboard & Rishi AI Chat (Supabase)

Two interactive features — the Rishi AI chat assistant and the global quiz leaderboard — use Supabase, a third-party backend service, for sign-in, temporary chat processing, and leaderboard storage. These features are optional; you can use most of the site, including playing the quiz for your own on-device stats, without signing in at all.

3.1 Signing In

To use Rishi AI chat or to appear on the global (cross-device) leaderboard, you can sign in one of two ways:

  • Google sign-in: Uses Google/Supabase authentication and requests only your email address as an identifier (via the userinfo.email scope). We do not request your contacts, files, or other Google account data.
  • Anonymous/demo sign-in: Creates a temporary, random session identifier with no personal information required.

Supabase issues and manages the resulting session on our behalf. We do not separately store your Google password — authentication is handled by Google and Supabase, not by us directly.

3.2 Quiz Leaderboard Data

If you enter a display name and complete the daily quiz while signed in, the following may be stored via Supabase and used to power the optional global leaderboard: your chosen display name, your account/session identifier, quiz streak, best score, total score, play count, and the date you last played. Your display name and score-related stats may be visible to other visitors on the public leaderboard; we do not display your email address there.

3.3 Rishi AI Chat

If you use Rishi AI chat, the message you send and a short portion of your recent conversation (limited to your current browser session) are sent to a backend function (hosted via Supabase) in order to generate a reply. We use this chat feature to provide AI-assisted responses; we do not use chat content for advertising.

Supabase is a third-party infrastructure provider. We do not control Supabase's internal infrastructure, data centers, or default retention schedule, and we do not make guarantees on Supabase's behalf. If you'd like a chat history or leaderboard entry removed, contact us at ay5890573@gmail.com.

4. Locally Stored Preferences (Browser Storage)

Certain features store information only in your own browser's local storage, on your device — this data is not automatically sent to us. This includes:

  • Your selected site language and light/dark theme preference
  • Per-device quiz statistics: high score, streak, play count, and whether you've already played today's quiz
  • A local counter used by an on-site mantra/japa counter feature

You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's site data for hinduresearch.com. Note that if you sign in and play the leaderboard-linked quiz, some of these same figures (streak, best score) are also synced to Supabase as described in Section 3.2.

5. Google Search Console

We use Google Search Console, linked to our Google Analytics property, purely as a site-owner tool to monitor how the site appears in Google Search — for example, search visibility, indexing status, and search performance. This linkage is a reporting convenience for us as site owners and does not, by itself, add any extra tracking of visitors beyond what is described in Section 2.

6. Cookies

Hindu Research Portal uses cookies and similar identifiers associated with the technologies actually running on the site — principally the first-party cookies set by Google Analytics (Section 2) and the session identifiers used by Supabase for sign-in (Section 3). We do not currently run advertising cookies or third-party ad-tracking scripts. If additional technologies that use cookies are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect them.

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings (see Section 9, Your Choices).

7. Contact Form & Formspree

Our contact form is processed using Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, which delivers your submission to our official inbox. If you submit the contact form, the information you voluntarily provide — such as your name, email address, subject, inquiry type, and message — is processed by Formspree in order to deliver it to us.

We do not control Formspree's internal infrastructure, data centers, or retention schedule, and we do not make guarantees on Formspree's behalf. Please refer to Formspree's own documentation for details: https://help.formspree.io/

8. Third-Party Links

Our pages may link to external websites (for example, source references, government portals, academic resources, or a WhatsApp share link) that are not operated by us. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any external site you visit.

9. Children's Privacy

Hindu Research Portal is intended for general educational use and is not specifically directed at young children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

10. Your Choices

You can manage your privacy using standard browser and device tools, as well as feature-level choices on this site, including:

  • Blocking or deleting cookies in your browser settings
  • Using your browser's "Do Not Track" or privacy/incognito modes
  • Opting out of Google Analytics tracking using Google's official Analytics opt-out browser add-on
  • Disabling location permissions for this site if you do not wish to use the Panchang location feature
  • Simply not signing in — Rishi AI chat and the global leaderboard are optional, and the quiz can be played for local, on-device stats without an account
  • Clearing your browser's local storage for this site to remove locally saved preferences and quiz stats
  • Contacting us to request removal of a leaderboard entry or chat-related data (Section 3)

11. Security

We take reasonable, practical steps to help protect information submitted through this site, including relying on the security practices of established providers (Google, Supabase, Formspree) for the features that use them. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Data Retention

We do not maintain a fixed, published retention schedule for analytics, sign-in, leaderboard, chat, or form-submission data at this time; such data is generally retained only as long as reasonably useful for the purposes described above, subject to the default settings of the third-party tools we use (Google Analytics, Supabase, Formspree).

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if we introduce new features, analytics tools, or (in the future) advertising or other third-party technologies. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ay5890573@gmail.com.
