Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Hindu Research Portal (accessible from hinduresearch.com), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Hindu Research Portal and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our portal, we may collect minimal data necessary to provide seamless services, such as:

  • Location Data: Used strictly on your device browser to calculate accurate real-time local Panchang timings (Sunrise, Sunset, Rahukaal). We do not store or sell your precise location.
  • Usage Data: Basic browser information, device type, and page interaction data for analytics and performance optimization.
  • User Accounts: Email address and authentication details if you sign up to track quiz scores or access saved features.

2. Log Files & Analytics

Hindu Research Portal follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Hindu Research Portal uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Advertising

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to hinduresearch.com and other sites on the internet.

Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Third Party Privacy Policies

Hindu Research Portal's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

6. CCPA & GDPR Privacy Rights

We respect your privacy rights. Users have the right to request access to their personal data, request correction or deletion, and restrict processing. If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you.

7. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@hinduresearch.com.
